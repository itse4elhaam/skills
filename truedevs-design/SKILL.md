# TrueDevs design

Open the [visual reference](references/visuals.html) before drawing anything.

## Look

Set type in Poppins, the site font. Ink is #23313c on warm white #fbfaf7, with secondary text in #54616b, clay #b04d2b for accents and links, sage #506358 for quiet marks, and #dadbd4 for hairlines. These are local reference values. When the site already has a token for a colour, use the token. Keep the type scale and component shapes the board already uses.

| Element | Reference size |
| --- | --- |
| Section heading | Poppins 500, 26–40 px, line height 1.13 |
| Body copy | Poppins 400, 13–16 px, line height 1.65–1.7 |
| Client portraits | 40 px with 12 px overlap on desktop, 31 px with 10 px overlap on a phone |

A real screen from client work goes first whenever it shows the point, such as a booking page before and after a rebuild. For a process or a time window, draw a restrained diagram from paper, lines and ranges, like a shaded strip of weeks or two plain panels.

## Approved homepage details

- **Client proof under the headline.** Compact overlapping circular owner photos. Picking a photo switches a verbatim quote attributed by first name and company, with “Watch their video reviews” below the quote. Keep the selected owner visible on keyboard focus.
- **Customer journey.** The heading is "How do your customers book or buy?" Four numbered circles with a search, click, card and parcel icon read They find you, They choose, They pay and You deliver. Use four columns on desktop and a two-by-two grid on a phone.

- **How we work. H1, One line, four books.** The four steps run along a single line, left to right. Each sits beside a small book-shaped picture of the real work at that step.

## Launch language

**About. W2, Release card and team.** A large 4–6 set in type on a small card, next to the four real team portraits.

The current decision is "first version to launch". Use 4 to 6 weeks as the team's planning target for a scoped first release of the part customers use first. Tie the next improvement to what real customers do, such as where someone stops on a booking or picks up the phone to call. Leave out staffing promises and invented weekly milestones.

Ask how customers book today. Name the remaining setup and the time it takes the owner. Explain what the team can learn from real visitors during the next busy period. Keep private calls anonymous in public copy.

## Review boards

Compare options at the same width. Option tabs, captions, compare and choose buttons and the desktop and phone toggles belong to the board. They must lift out cleanly and leave a section that could ship as it stands. Check every option at phone width. Text stays readable and long rows stack within the page.
